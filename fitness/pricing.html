Common Questions

The honest answers.

Can I freeze my membership?

+
Yes. Monthly members can freeze for up to 30 days per year; Annual members up to 60 days. Freezes are approved for medical reasons, travel, or personal circumstances. Just email us at least 5 days in advance. No fees, no judgment.

What's the cancellation policy?

+
Monthly memberships require 30 days written notice. Annual memberships are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person at any time — just let us know. We try to be human about this. If something big comes up, reach out and we'll work something out.

Are guest passes included with membership?

+
Monthly members get 1 guest pass per month. Annual members get 2 per month. Drop-in members can purchase guest passes at the front desk for $25 each. Guest passes expire at the end of the month — they don't roll over.

What do I need for my first class?

+
Just comfortable clothes and a water bottle. We provide mats, blocks, bolsters, reformers, boxing gloves, and all equipment. Grip socks are required for Barre, Pilates, and Reformer classes — we sell them at the front desk if you don't have a pair yet.

Is the 7-day trial really free? No credit card?

+
Genuinely free. No credit card required to start. We'll ask for payment information when your trial ends if you decide to continue — but we never auto-charge without your confirmation. We'd rather earn your membership than trick you into it.
