About Us

Modern Care, Personal Touch

Founded in 2009, Bright Smile Dental has been serving families in the community with quality dental care built on trust and compassion. Our state-of-the-art facility features the latest in digital imaging, laser dentistry, and patient comfort technology.

We believe everyone deserves a smile they're proud of. That's why we offer flexible payment options, accept most insurance plans, and create an environment where even the most anxious patients feel at ease.
