Meet Our Team

The people behind your smile. We're a small team that genuinely cares about our patients and loves what we do.

Our Doctors

Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DDS

Lead Dentist & Owner
University of Michigan, 2008 Cosmetic Specialist Invisalign Certified Provider

Dr. Mitchell founded Bright Smile in 2011 after 3 years at a large corporate dental group. She wanted to build a practice where patients felt like family, not a number. Her passion for cosmetic dentistry and commitment to continuing education means she's always bringing the latest techniques to her patients. She's completed over 100 Invisalign cases and regularly attends advanced training in smile design.

Fun fact: Avid marathon runner — ask about her Boston Marathon times. She'll also make sure your jaw is in good enough shape for all that post-race celebrating.

Dr. James Park, DMD

Associate Dentist
Tufts University, 2015 Pediatric Dentistry Restorative Care

Dr. Park joined in 2018 and quickly became our patients' favorite for his gentle approach, especially with kids and anxious patients. His calm demeanor and clear communication style put even the most nervous patients at ease. Before joining Bright Smile, Dr. Park completed an advanced residency in general practice at Boston Medical Center, where he developed his skills in treating medically complex patients.

Fun fact: Speaks fluent Korean and always has candy at his station (sugar-free, of course — he's still a dentist).
