Our Services

From routine checkups to complete smile transformations. Transparent pricing, modern technology, and a gentle touch every visit.

Preventive & Restorative

General Dentistry

The foundation of a healthy smile. We focus on prevention first and conservative treatment when needed. Regular visits help us catch issues early, when they're simplest and most affordable to fix.

Cleanings & Exams

$150 – $200

Professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar that brushing misses. Combined with a thorough examination, this is the cornerstone of dental health and early detection.

What to expect: 45-60 min appointment. Cleaning, exam, oral cancer screening, and discussion of any concerns. X-rays taken annually or as needed.

Fillings

$180 – $350

Tooth-colored composite fillings restore decayed teeth to their natural look and function. We use BPA-free materials that bond directly to your tooth structure for a strong, invisible repair.

What to expect: 30-45 min. Local anesthetic, decay removal, filling placement, and bite adjustment. Most patients return to normal activity immediately.

Crowns

$900 – $1,400

Custom-made ceramic crowns protect and restore damaged or weakened teeth. Our same-day CEREC technology means many crowns can be done in a single visit with no temporary needed.

What to expect: 1-2 visits depending on case complexity. Digital scan (no messy impressions), tooth preparation, and crown placement with precise color matching.

Root Canals

$700 – $1,100

Save an infected tooth instead of extracting it. Modern root canal therapy is comfortable and efficient, eliminating pain and preserving your natural smile for years to come.

What to expect: 60-90 min. Local anesthetic ensures comfort throughout. Infected tissue removal, cleaning, and sealing. Follow-up crown usually recommended.

Extractions

$200 – $500

When a tooth can't be saved, gentle extraction with proper planning. We discuss replacement options (implants, bridges) to maintain your smile and prevent neighboring teeth from shifting.

What to expect: 30-60 min depending on complexity. Local anesthetic, gentle extraction, aftercare instructions. Most patients manage with over-the-counter pain relief.

Smile Makeovers

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile is the first thing people notice. Whether you want whiter teeth, a straighter smile, or a complete transformation, we combine art and science for results that look naturally beautiful.

Teeth Whitening

$350 – $500

Professional-grade whitening that's dramatically more effective than store-bought kits. Choose in-office treatment for instant results or custom take-home trays for gradual brightening.

What to expect: In-office: 60 min, up to 8 shades lighter in one visit. Take-home: custom trays worn 30 min/day for 2 weeks. Sensitivity is minimal and temporary.

Porcelain Veneers

$800 – $1,500/tooth

Ultra-thin porcelain shells that transform the shape, color, and alignment of your teeth. Veneers are the gold standard for creating a flawless, natural-looking smile.

What to expect: 2 visits over 2-3 weeks. Consultation with digital smile preview, minimal preparation, and final bonding. Results last 10-15+ years with good care.

Dental Bonding

$250 – $600

A quick, affordable way to fix chips, gaps, and discoloration. Tooth-colored composite resin is sculpted directly onto your teeth for an immediate improvement in a single visit.

What to expect: 30-60 min per tooth. No anesthetic needed in most cases. Composite is shaped, hardened, and polished to match your natural teeth.

Invisalign

$3,500 – $6,000

Straighten your teeth discreetly with clear, removable aligners. Dr. Mitchell is a certified Invisalign provider with over 100 cases completed. Eat what you want, smile with confidence throughout treatment.

What to expect: 6-18 months depending on case. 3D scan, custom treatment plan, new aligners every 1-2 weeks. Checkups every 6-8 weeks. Retainer included.

When You Need Us Most

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies don't wait for business hours. Whether it's a sudden toothache, a broken tooth, or a knocked-out filling, we're here for you with same-day appointments and walk-in availability.

Same-Day Emergency

Call us and we'll fit you in today. We reserve time slots specifically for emergency patients so you're never left waiting in pain. Walk-ins are also welcome during business hours.

Common emergencies: Severe toothache, cracked/broken tooth, lost crown or filling, abscess/swelling, knocked-out tooth, broken braces wire.

After-Hours Line

Our emergency line is answered 24/7. If you're in pain outside business hours, call (312) 555-0235 and one of our doctors will call you back within 30 minutes with guidance or to arrange immediate care.

Before you call: If you've knocked out a tooth, place it in milk and come in immediately. For severe bleeding or facial trauma, go to your nearest ER first.

Dental Emergency?

Don't wait. Call our emergency line anytime — day or night. We'll get you out of pain and protect your smile.
