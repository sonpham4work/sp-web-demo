Welcome

Where tradition meets the bowl

Every bowl at Soba House starts with broth simmered for 18 hours, noodles pulled fresh that morning, and ingredients sourced from Pacific Northwest farms. Chef Kenji Takahashi brings eight years of training in Tokyo's finest ramen shops to every dish we serve.

We believe great noodles don't need shortcuts. Just patience, craft, and the best ingredients we can find.
