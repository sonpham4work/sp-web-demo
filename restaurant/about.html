Our Story

More than a noodle shop

Eight years of training. Six years of serving. One obsession.

From Tokyo to Portland

Founded by Chef Kenji Takahashi in 2018, Soba House started as a 12-seat noodle bar in the Arts District. Kenji spent 8 years training in Tokyo's best ramen shops before bringing his craft to Portland. Today we serve 200+ bowls daily, each made with the same care as the first.

What started as a tiny counter with four stools and a dream became something bigger — not because we chased growth, but because people kept coming back. They came for the broth. They stayed for the feeling of being somewhere that genuinely cares about every detail in every bowl.

"Ramen is patience made edible. You can't rush an 18-hour broth. You can't shortcut fresh noodles. The bowl knows."
— Chef Kenji Takahashi
What We Believe

Our Principles

麺

Fresh Noodles Daily

Every noodle is made in-house each morning. We mix, knead, rest, and cut — never from a package, never from yesterday.

汁

18-Hour Broth

Our tonkotsu starts simmering at 3am. Pork bones, aromatics, and time — there's no substitute for patience.

畑

Local Ingredients

We partner with Oregon farms for seasonal produce, Willamette Valley eggs, and Carlton Farms pork.

心

Zero Additives

No MSG, no artificial flavors, no shortcuts. Just real food made by real people who care deeply about the craft.

Our Journey

Milestones

2018
The Beginning Opened a 12-seat noodle bar in Portland's Arts District with a $30k budget and a dream. Sold out on day one.
2019
Portland Mercury Award Named "Best New Restaurant" by Portland Mercury. Lines started forming around the block every weekend.
2020
The Pivot COVID hit hard. We pivoted to takeout and meal kits, selling 150+ ramen kits per week. The community kept us alive.
2022
New Home Expanded to our current 60-seat location on NW Davis Street. Custom noodle-making station visible from every seat.
2024
National Recognition Featured in Bon Appétit's "Best Ramen Outside Japan" list. Chef Kenji profiled in the November issue.
