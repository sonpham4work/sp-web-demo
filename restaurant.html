Our Story

Where Tradition Meets the Neighbourhood

Soba House started with a simple idea: serve the kind of noodles we grew up eating, in a space where everyone feels welcome. Our recipes are rooted in Japanese tradition, but our kitchen is inspired by what's fresh and local.

We make our soba and udon from scratch every morning. Our broths simmer for over 12 hours. It's a lot of work, but you can taste the difference — and that's the whole point.

— Yuki & Hana Tanaka, Founders
