The Story Behind the Studio

From Monday meetings
to wet fur and happy dogs.

I spent a decade in corporate marketing. Good pay, genuinely smart people, and absolutely terrible Mondays. I was fine — I just wasn't really present. The only time I felt like myself was walking my dogs, or volunteering at the shelter on weekends.

"The only time I felt truly present was when I was with my dogs. In 2019, I quit my job, enrolled in grooming school, and apprenticed for two years under a master groomer in Asheville."

Puddle Lane opened in 2021 in a tiny shop on Maple Street. I named it after the street where I grew up — my parents let every neighborhood dog into the yard. It always smelled like wet fur and happiness. That's the energy I was going for.

Today I only take a limited number of appointments each week. That's on purpose. I'd rather do 5 things right than 20 things fast. The dogs know the difference, even if the accounting spreadsheet doesn't.

How We Work

Our Philosophy

🚫

No Crates. Ever.

Your dog is never put in a kennel while they wait or dry. They roam the studio freely, because that's what a stress-free visit looks like.

🐕

One Dog in the Salon

We don't double-book. One dog at a time means full attention, zero chaos, and a dog who doesn't have to compete for space or calm.

📱

We Text When They're Ready

You'll get a text the moment we're done. We ask that you pick up within 5 minutes — your dog isn't going to sit in a cage waiting for you.

💚

Fear Free Certified

Every groomer on the team is trained in Fear Free techniques — reading body language, managing stress, and creating a session your dog actually tolerates (sometimes enjoys).

🩺

We Always Tell You

If we notice something — a skin irregularity, a lump, an ear that smells off — we'll mention it. We're not vets, but we've got eyes, and we use them. Your dog's health is part of the job.
