The Person Behind the Scissors

Hi, I'm Rachel.

"I left my marketing job in 2019 because I kept daydreaming about dogs."

12 years, one certification, and one studio later — here we are. Puddle Lane is named after the street where I grew up, where every neighborhood dog had a standing invitation to the yard.

I only take a limited number of appointments each week. That's on purpose. I'd rather do 5 things right than 20 things fast.
