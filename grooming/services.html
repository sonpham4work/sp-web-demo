The Experience

What to Expect

From the moment you walk in to the "your pup is ready" text — here's how a visit goes.

👋

Drop-Off & Meet

Give yourself 10 minutes at drop-off. We want to meet your dog, learn their quirks, and make them comfortable before we ever touch a brush. No rushed handoffs here.

🔍

Pre-Groom Assessment

We check coat condition, skin, ears, nails, and any areas of concern. If we notice anything your vet should know about, we'll tell you. This is a health check as much as a style consult.

🛁

Bath Time

Warm water, a shampoo suited to their coat type, and a conditioning treatment. No rush — we let the products do their job properly.

💨

Dry & Brush-Out

Hand-held dryer only — no dryer boxes or kennels. We brush while drying so the coat lies exactly right before any cutting begins.

✂️

Cut & Style (Full Groom)

Breed-standard or custom. We work slowly and let your dog take breaks if needed. This is not a race — it's a craft.

✨

Finishing Touches

Nail trim, ear clean, paw pad moisturizer, spritz. If they got a full groom: bandana or bow. Then we text you — and you have five minutes to come get them. No waiting in a cage.

🏡

Pickup

We'll share what we noticed, what went well, and anything to keep an eye on. Your dog gets a treat on the way out. See you next time.

Booking & Cancellation Policy

We hold your spot just for you — please give 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. We totally understand that life happens.

A 50% no-show fee applies to missed appointments without notice. This helps us support the groomers who set aside that time.
