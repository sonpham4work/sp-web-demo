Key Findings
HIGH
Checkout abandonment at 73%
Cart-to-checkout flow has 5 steps. Industry best practice is 2-3. Users drop off at shipping address entry (no autofill support). Recommendation: consolidate to 2-step checkout with address autocomplete.
HIGH
Mobile page load > 6 seconds
Unoptimized product images (avg 2.4MB each) and render-blocking scripts cause severe mobile delays. First contentful paint at 4.2s. Recommendation: implement lazy loading, WebP format, and defer non-critical JS.
MEDIUM
No search functionality on catalog page
450+ products with only category browsing. Users with specific intent leave within 8 seconds. Adding search with autocomplete could reduce bounce rate by ~20%.
LOW
Missing structured data markup
Product pages lack JSON-LD schema. Adding Product, Review, and BreadcrumbList markup would enable rich snippets in search results, estimated +15% organic CTR.
